# AI Employee Business FAQ & Knowledge Base

## 1. Store & Office Hours
- **Monday to Friday:** 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (BST / UTC+6)
- **Saturday:** 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
- **Sunday:** Closed (Emergency support available via phone escalation)
- **Public Holidays:** Closed unless prior enterprise appointment is confirmed.

## 2. Services & Pricing
- **Initial Business Consultation:** $50 (or 5,000 BDT) for a 45-minute session.
- **Standard AI Receptionist Setup:** $199/month - includes 24/7 bilingual (English & Bangla) customer support, lead capture, and appointment booking.
- **Enterprise AI Employee Suite:** $499/month - includes both AI Receptionist and AI Business Analyst, custom knowledge base ingestion, CRM integration, and weekly executive reports.
- **Custom Voice & Telephony Integration:** One-time setup fee of $150 (excluding Twilio/SIP carrier usage costs).

## 3. Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy
- **Subscription Refund Policy:** Full 14-day money-back guarantee for all new monthly subscriptions if cancelled within 14 calendar days of initial activation.
- **Appointment Rescheduling & Cancellation:** Customers may reschedule or cancel booked consultations free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time.
- **Late Cancellations:** Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled slot incur a $15 administrative fee.

## 4. Escalation & Human Support
- If a customer reports a billing dispute, urgent technical outage, or explicitly asks to speak with the business owner, the AI Receptionist immediately logs a high-priority lead and transfers or escalates the conversation to a human manager.
